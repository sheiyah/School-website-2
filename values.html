WHAT WE BELIEVE

Our Values
& principles.

01 / MAIN VALUES

Core pillars shaping our community.

01

Accountability

At Alashbal International School, accountability is essential for building a trustworthy and reliable environment. This category emphasizes the importance of taking responsibility for actions and decisions.

02

Innovation

Innovation is at the core of Alashbal International School’s approach to education, preparing students to adapt and thrive in a rapidly changing world. This category focuses on fostering creativity and adaptability.

03

Service

Service is a fundamental value at Alashbal International School, reflecting a commitment to making a positive impact on the community and the world. This category emphasizes empathy, teamwork, and global responsibility.

02 / EDUCATIONAL VALUES

Principles that guide our students daily.

01

Honesty

To tell the truth and uphold truthfulness in all actions and communications.

02

Respect

Being considerate and honoring others, valuing diversity and personal boundaries.

03

Social Responsibility

Accountable for fulfilling one's civil duty and contributing positively to society.

04

Gratitude

The quality of being thankful and appreciating the blessings and efforts of others.

05

Positivity

The state of being positive, optimistic, and bringing constructive energy to challenges.

06

Initiative

To take action and do things proactively without needing to be told.

07

Confidence

Feeling secure of yourself, your character, and your capabilities.

08

Forgiveness

The ability to let go of resentment and forgive mistakes graciously.

09

Humility

Being humble, modest, and always open to learning from everyone.

10

Integrity

Being honest and having strong moral principles that you refuse to compromise.
