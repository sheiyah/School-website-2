STUDENT CLUBS & DIPLOMACY

Model United
Nations (MUN) Club.

01 / OUR MISSION & PURPOSE

Developing future diplomats and global thinkers.

The Model United Nations (MUN) Club at Alashbal International School empowers students to simulate delegates of the United Nations and explore complex international issues. Members research global challenges, draft resolutions, and debate pressing world affairs.

By engaging in conferences and debates, our delegates foster public speaking, critical negotiation skills, and global awareness, preparing them for international leadership roles.

Core Objectives

  • Mastering diplomacy, negotiation, and parliamentary procedure.
  • Building critical research and analytical writing skills.
  • Participating in local and international MUN conferences.
  • Promoting cross-cultural understanding and awareness.

02 / CLUB ACTIVITIES

Engaging in global debate and leadership.

01

Debate & Public Speaking

Weekly practice sessions focusing on persuasive speeches, moderated caucuses, and resolution drafting skills.

02

MUN Conferences

Representing AIS at prestigious regional and international student-led conferences throughout the year.

03

Global Affairs Research

Investigating geopolitics, human rights, and environmental sustainability to form policy position papers.
