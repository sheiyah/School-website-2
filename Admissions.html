02 / ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS

What you will need to apply.

To ensure a smooth registration process, please prepare the following documentation prior to completing your application:

  • Copy of student's passport and valid QID / visa
  • Copy of parents' passport and valid QID
  • Recent passport-sized photographs of the student
  • Official academic report cards from previous 2 school years
  • Up-to-date immunization and health records

03 / GET IN TOUCH

Have questions about admissions?

Our admissions department is here to support you at every stage of the application process. Feel free to reach out to arrange a consultation or tour.
