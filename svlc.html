STUDENT LIFE & LEADERSHIP

Student Voice
Leadership Council (SVLC).

01 / OUR MISSION & PURPOSE

Empowering cubs to lead with purpose and integrity.

The Student Voice Leadership Council (SVLC) serves as the primary representative body for students at Alashbal International School. We bridge the gap between the student body and school administration, ensuring every cub's voice is heard, valued, and acted upon.

Through collaborative initiatives, community service, and school-wide events, our council members cultivate essential leadership skills that prepare them to become global leaders of tomorrow.

Core Objectives

  • Enhancing student welfare and campus experience.
  • Organizing academic and extracurricular activities.
  • Fostering an inclusive and supportive school culture.
  • Promoting active citizenship and peer mentoring.

02 / COUNCIL INITIATIVES

Making a positive impact across campus.

01

Peer Support & Mentorship

Senior council members guide new students, helping them transition smoothly into the Cambridge curriculum and school community.

02

Campus Events & Assemblies

Planning student assemblies, cultural showcases, talent spotlight events, and charity fundraisers throughout the academic year.

03

Administration Liaison

Holding regular open-forum meetings with school leadership to communicate student feedback and propose innovative campus enhancements.
