02 / OUR STORY & VISION

Enriching institutions with exceptional taste & nutrition.

Jazz Cafe was founded in 2015 by two dedicated entrepreneurs, Jassim M Al Theyab and Jeronimo D'Souza. It began out of a mutual love for exceptional food, specialty aromatic coffee, and delicate pastries.

Our vision is to enrich institutions by providing high-quality, nutritious, and delicious meals for both students and staff every single day.

Operations & Reach

  • Specialized in modern, reliable, and smooth-running school cafeterias.
  • Serving thousands of students daily across major campuses in Doha.
  • Proud catering partners for the American School of Doha (ASD) and Qatar-Finland International School (QFIS).
  • Neighborhood bakery flagship located on Al Mishaal Street.
