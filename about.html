05 / STUDENT COMMITMENT

Anti-Bullying Pledge.

As a student of Alashbal International School, I pledge to be a champion of kindness and respect in our school community. I understand that my actions and words have a powerful impact, and I commit to using them to build others up, not tear them down.

1. Respect Everyone
Treat every peer and adult with dignity, celebrating our differences and learning from one another.

2. Speak Up
Report bullying or harassment immediately if I witness it or experience it myself.

3. Support My Peers
Offer support to those who are bullied or feel alone.

4. Embrace Diversity
Learn about people from different backgrounds and cultures.

5. Practice Kindness
Be proactive in creating a positive and welcoming school environment.

6. Follow School Rules
Adhere to the school's anti-bullying policies and community values.
